Breads Bakery Academy August 2026

Project Timeline & Scope of Work

Project Overview

The goal of all four modules is to create a system where Breads’ standards are never lost, never inconsistent, and never dependent on any one person being in the room — so that every employee, at every location, on every shift, is working from the same playbook.

Scope of Work

1 Module 1

Creating a Single Source of Truth Knowledge Base

We start by ingesting your existing training materials and SOPs into a structured, AI-queryable knowledge base. There is one consistent and correct answer per topic. When something is updated, the old version is archived and the new one goes live. Through a simple, branded mobile webpage, any employee at any location can ask any operational question and get one consistent, correct answer pulled directly from your standards.

2 Module 2

Intaking New SOPs and Flagging Deviations

Through a dedicated email address for you and your inner team, you can walk into a location, spot something, send a photo and a note. It gets added into the knowledge base — either creating a new standard or flagging an update to an existing one for your review.

3 Module 3

Communicating Updated SOPs in an Interactive Format

Every week, a short multiple-choice quiz is drawn from your knowledge base and pushed to each location’s Slack channel. Team members respond directly in Slack — no new app, no new login. New and updated standards are reinforced consistently across all six locations.

4 Module 4

Evaluating Team Training Performance

All quiz responses are logged automatically: who answered, what they answered, whether it was correct, by location and role. Every question employees ask the Breads Bakery Academy agent is also logged. Through a reporting view, you can track compliance rates, scores by location, and the most common knowledge gaps across your team.
